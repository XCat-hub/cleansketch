How It Works

    Create a canvas

    Draw

    Use layers, pens, shapes, fill, and particle brushes. Undo, redo, and organize pages as you go.

  3. 3

    Export or back up

    Save a high-quality export to your device, share your work, or optionally back up drawings to Google Drive.

FAQ

Common questions about CleanSketch.

Do I need a Google account?

No. You can draw locally without signing in. A Google account is required for Drive backup, restoring a purchase, and some account features.

What is CleanSketch Pro?

Pro is a one-time Google Play purchase. It removes ads and unlocks custom canvas sizes and all particle effect brushes. There is no subscription and no auto-renewal.

Are there ads?

The free version may show app-open ads. You can also watch a rewarded ad for one day of Pro. Pro users do not see ads.

Where is my artwork stored?

Drawings are stored on your device by default. If you sign in and enable backup, packages are uploaded to your own Google Drive app-data folder. We do not use your drawings for AI generation.

Which languages are supported?

The app supports 15+ languages including English, Chinese (Simplified, Traditional, and Hong Kong), Japanese, Korean, Arabic, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Italian, Russian, Indonesian, Hindi, Thai, Tamil, Bengali, and Urdu.
